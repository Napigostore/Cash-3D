Cash to Freedom is a 3D board game about the money decisions of real life. Start with a job, a salary and a few bills, then roll the dice, take opportunities and build passive income until it covers your expenses. That is the moment you escape the Grind Mill and enter the Prosperity Track, where you invest big to buy your dream.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT
• Real-life finance, simplified: salary every 4 months, taxes, inflation, mortgages, teaser rates that expire, market booms and freezes.
• Learn by doing: after every purchase you drag each item into Assets, Liabilities, Income or Expenses. Get it wrong and you fix it before moving on.
• You reap what you sow: six life stats (Happiness, Luck, Health, Kids, Investing knowledge, Connections) change the odds of your future. Charity raises Luck; at 80 Luck your next roll is doubled.
• Choices with trade-offs: public or international school, scooter or a luxury SUV, budget trip or 5-star resort, marry or stay single.
• Opportunities grow with you: side jobs and small shops first, then apartments, café chains, factories and company buyouts.
• Golden Rooster, your advisor: a limited number of hints, plus a post-game review of your strengths and weaknesses as an investor.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Easy mode to learn, Hard mode that feels like life: a smart player escapes around age 45–50, most never do.
• Play solo against bots (Cautious, Balanced, Risky) or pass the phone with friends.
• 12 careers from factory worker to pilot, 8 dreams, 12 zodiac tokens in 7 colors.
• Animated 3D board with a growing city of the assets you own, mood-adaptive music.
• English and Vietnamese.

CASH TO FREEDOM PRO (optional, one-time purchase)
• The Golden Rooster becomes your personal financial expert: unlimited questions and deeper analysis (fair value of each deal, debt ratio, asset allocation, your next concrete step).
• Post-game review of your strengths and weaknesses as an investor.
• VIP life: start with +10 Luck and Happiness, a crown on the leaderboard, slightly better deals.

No ads. No account. Works offline.

All companies, stocks, prices and projects in the game are fictional simulations for learning, not investment advice.
